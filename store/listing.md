## Category
Games → Card, or Games → Role Playing (pick whichever fits Play Console's
current taxonomy best at submission time)

## Content rating
Run the Play Console questionnaire honestly. This game has: no real violence
(stylized card damage numbers only), no blood/gore, no user-generated content,
no real-money purchases, no ads currently. Should land at "Everyone" or
"Everyone 10+" depending on how the questionnaire treats fantasy combat themes.

## Privacy policy
Required even for apps that collect no data. Since this app currently makes
no network calls, stores no personal data, and has no accounts/login, the
policy can be very short — see store/privacy-policy.md for a starting draft.
You still need to host it somewhere public (a GitHub Pages page, a simple
webpage, even a gist) and link that URL in Play Console.

## Screenshots
See store/screenshots/ — 4 phone screenshots at 1080x1920 (compliant with
Play Store's required aspect ratio, min 320px/max 3840px per side).
Play Console requires a minimum of 2; these 4 cover: hunter select, a hunter's
detail/kit view, trial start, and mid-battle action.
